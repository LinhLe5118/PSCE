\section{Sealed Evaluation Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}
The seventh cohort contains 120 new kernels: 84 stochastic MDPs from Garnet, forward-layered, and bottleneck families and 36 deterministic lock controls. Kernels and seeds were checked against opened cohorts before outcomes. Five behavior policies, three core learners (CFQI, IQL, behavior cloning), six budgets, and ten training seeds produce 108,000 core learner jobs. The 84 stochastic MDPs form the primary panel; the deterministic panel is descriptive. The forecaster was fit on seven previously opened cohorts. Neither seventh-cohort learner outcomes nor oracle MDP descriptors entered its pilot-only predictions. The protocol and prediction files were sealed before outcome training. Archived pre-outcome amendments document schema compatibility, resolved kernel seeds, reused text IDs, and the selector overlap guard; they did not change the decision rule.

For the two forecast metrics, a seventh-cohort registered contrast requires positive mean improvement and a strictly positive simultaneous one-sided 95\% lower bound. This protocol has no 5\% minimum; the earlier fourth-cohort protocol did, and its verdict is retained separately. Inference resamples whole MDPs with a max-$t$ correction over the registered comparisons. Tier 1 contains the architecture-matched B2 pilot baseline and B2 plus data-only ERI and BWD adaptations, along with generic B2 and quality/coverage adaptations fitted with AFT only and an unfitted train-frequency row. Tier 2 contains true-MDP oracle EH and author-code TQ/SACo features with privileged reference datasets. The all-comparator verdict includes both tiers, but shared gate membership does not make their architectures or information budgets identical. The selection gate, prespecified separately, compares normalized return and seed success against fixed CFQI; the best fixed learner and v5 are reported separately. No threshold or comparator was removed after seeing outcomes.

These are new MDP instances, not a new environment family. A new kernel within an existing generator tests within-family transfer. It cannot establish transfer to an independently designed tabular benchmark. A separate Farama Minari FourRooms offline dataset is used only for a pre-registered Dirichlet transition-prediction component test, because its local visual observations are partial. Scores and confidence bounds are averaged and resampled at the base-MDP level, avoiding an artificial sample size from the five behaviors and ten seeds of one MDP.
