\section{BRIDGE follow-up for OEH-F (descriptive)}\label{sec:bridge-oehf}
These BRIDGE panels predate the final method. The forecaster scored here is \textbf{OEH-F}, the full 108-field head without the shift gate; the final, shift-gated OEH was not run on BRIDGE. The prospective five-task panel was sealed before its labels. The 14 MiniGrid tasks had been opened before OEH-F was run on them, so that panel is post-outcome exploratory. All intervals are descriptive.
