\section{Exploratory Block Ablation on Cohort 7}
\label{supp:ablation}
The block ablation refits the matched head on four column subsets defined from the 108-column lineage: B2 alone; without direct OEH-B (G2, G3, G6; OEH-H still uses the posterior mean); without OEH-H and the risk profile (G4, G5); and without the pilot diagnostics D and their context (G7, G8). The full and B2 arms reproduce the sealed predictions to within $10^{-7}$. An audit hook blocked every test-outcome file until all predictions were hashed. Because the cohort was already opened, the results in Table~\ref{tab:learner} are exploratory. Only a few effects have MDP-clustered intervals that exclude zero: removing direct OEH-B worsens the pooled LL ($+0.0098$ [$0.0016$, $0.0185$]) and both CFQI scores, and removing OEH-H worsens IQL's LL ($+0.0251$ [$0.0053$, $0.0464$]); removing OEH-H improves BC's mean IBS, and removing the pilot diagnostics D changes nothing detectable.
