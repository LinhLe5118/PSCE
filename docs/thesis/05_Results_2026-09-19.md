## 5.1 Evaluation boundary

This chapter reports development evidence only. The separate 120-MDP evaluation
split remained sealed because the pre-committed development objective did not
pass. The OEH-v3 formula, feature block, generator, seed namespaces, grouped
folds, and primary endpoint were frozen in commit
`e0eb32ca8a2812625affae266f59b3d5b3e78de6` before the cohort below was
generated.

The single-use post-freeze cohort contains 60 new MDPs: 16 uniform Garnets, 20
forward-layered MDPs, 16 lock-structured MDPs, and 8 bottleneck MDPs. Five
behavior-policy families produce 300 MDP--behavior groups. Pilot streams were
used only for features. Independent training streams produced the outcomes for
CFQI, tabular IQL, and tabular behavior cloning (BC). There is no instance ID or
seed overlap with the sealed evaluation split.

## 5.2 OEH-v3 removes the observed mechanical floor

The pilot-only OEH-v3 run completed 900 cases over pilot multipliers 2, 5, and
10, producing 90,900 depth rows with no errors. Across all pilot sizes, 699
cases had a selectable positive requirement and 201 were `epsilon_trivial`.
The run metadata records that learner outcomes, training buffers, and sealed
evaluation data were not accessed.

At the primary multiplier 10, all 300 groups had a finite selected profile. Of
these, 240 had positive requirements and 60 were `epsilon_trivial`. The 240
positive values contained 238 distinct values and ranged from 9,081.49 to
1.033 billion transitions. Thus OEH-v3 does not reproduce OEH-v1's common
finite floor or OEH-v2's near-total non-identification. This is a mechanical
result: variation in a forecast is necessary for prediction, but does not show
that the forecast predicts learner outcomes.

## 5.3 Learner outcomes

The independent training grid completed 54,000 fits: 18,000 each for CFQI,
IQL, and BC, with no invalid runs. Each method was evaluated at data
multipliers `{1,2,5,10,20,50}` and ten training seeds for every group.

CFQI showed the clearest response to data. Its median normalized return rose
from 0.668 at multiplier 1 to 0.972 at multiplier 50, while the corresponding
fit-level success rate rose from 0.207 to 0.568. A finite empirical requirement
was observed for 144 of 300 groups. IQL's median rose from 0.556 to 0.665 and 55
requirements were observed. BC remained near 0.28--0.32 and 28 requirements
were observed. Right censoring was therefore substantial, especially for IQL
and BC, and was handled by the discrete-time survival likelihood rather than by
dropping or imputing censored groups.

## 5.4 Does OEH-v3 improve prediction?

Five outer folds and four inner folds were grouped by base MDP. Standardization
and ridge selection occurred inside each training fold. The analysis produced
3,600 out-of-fold predictions: 300 groups times three learners times four
feature blocks.

| Learner | Features | Mean log-likelihood ↑ | Integrated Brier ↓ |
|---|---|---:|---:|
| CFQI | B0 | -1.4898 | 0.1552 |
| CFQI | B1 | -1.3476 | 0.1280 |
| CFQI | B2 | -1.1804 | 0.0943 |
| CFQI | B3 | -1.2009 | 0.0964 |
| IQL | B0 | -0.7536 | 0.1295 |
| IQL | B1 | -0.5368 | 0.0702 |
| IQL | B2 | -0.5464 | 0.0736 |
| IQL | B3 | -0.4954 | 0.0624 |
| BC | B0 | -0.5923 | 0.0687 |
| BC | B1 | -0.5833 | 0.0639 |
| BC | B2 | -0.5828 | 0.0638 |
| BC | B3 | -0.5024 | 0.0509 |

The frozen primary OEH endpoint pooled all learner panels. B3 minus B2 improved
mean log-likelihood by 0.03697, but its 95% MDP-cluster bootstrap interval
[-0.00853, 0.08158] included zero (`p=0.1118`). The primary gate therefore
failed. The secondary integrated-Brier contrast favored B3 by -0.00732 with a
95% interval [-0.01360, -0.00132] (`p=0.0152`). This secondary result is useful
evidence of better probability calibration over the budget grid, but it cannot
replace a failed pre-specified primary endpoint.

B2 minus B1 improved pooled log-likelihood by 0.05267 [0.01841, 0.08449] and
integrated Brier by -0.01012 [-0.01591, -0.00462]. Stochasticity and uncertainty
features therefore added pooled predictive information on this cohort. The
claim that specifically concerns OEH-v3 remains unsupported by the primary
test.

Per-learner results explain the pooled uncertainty. B3 slightly worsened both
metrics for CFQI. It improved both point estimates for IQL, but neither
Holm-adjusted interval excluded zero. For BC, B3 improved Brier loss after Holm
correction, while its log-likelihood improvement did not survive that family.
These are secondary analyses and do not redefine the endpoint after observing
the data.

## 5.5 Does prediction improve learner selection?

At budget `10|S||A|`, each model selected the learner with the highest
out-of-fold probability of success. The best-fixed comparator was chosen using
only the outer-training MDPs.

| Features | Selector return | Best-fixed | Oracle | Selector - best-fixed, 95% CI |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| B0 | 0.6463 | 0.6859 | 0.7101 | -0.0396 [-0.0650, -0.0175] |
| B1 | 0.6415 | 0.6859 | 0.7101 | -0.0445 [-0.0618, -0.0289] |
| B2 | 0.6270 | 0.6859 | 0.7101 | -0.0589 [-0.0841, -0.0371] |
| B3 | 0.6440 | 0.6859 | 0.7101 | -0.0420 [-0.0661, -0.0211] |

Every learned selector underperformed best-fixed. B3 recovered part of B2's
loss but did not exceed even B0. The learner-selection objective therefore
failed independently of the predictive primary endpoint.

## 5.6 Controlled decomposition of stochastic tabular data

The existing matched corridor factorial contains 810 cells formed from six
fixed topology--reward blocks. It varies transition slip, behavior decoy
probability, and data budget while holding the other indexed factors fixed.
This experiment separates three quantities that are often incorrectly grouped
under a single label of dataset difficulty.

| Intervention | Return difference | Coverage difference | Stochastic diagnostic difference |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Slip `0.20 - 0.00` | -0.0356 | -0.0294 | transition entropy +0.1800; stochastic-cell fraction +0.5081 |
| Behavior decoy `0.45 - 0.05` | -0.2440 | +0.0387 | transition entropy -0.0452 |
| Budget `50 - 2` | +0.2001 | +0.2687 | stochastic-cell fraction +0.0987 |

Increasing transition slip strongly changes measured stochasticity but has a
smaller average return effect than changing behavior quality. More strikingly,
the high-decoy behavior intervention slightly increases raw observed
state--action coverage while reducing CFQI return by 0.244. Coverage quantity
therefore cannot substitute for coverage of useful decisions. Increasing budget
improves both coverage and return, but it also changes which stochastic cells
can be detected from finite samples.

OEH-v2 resolution also illustrates the distinction between identifiability and
utility. The high-decoy intervention raises its resolved fraction by 0.2185
while learner return falls, whereas the larger budget raises resolution by
0.7148 and return by 0.2001. A diagnostic can become easier to compute without
the dataset becoming better for the learner.

These are descriptive matched effects. Six base topology--reward blocks are
too few for a general causal claim, and the factorial was observed before this
interpretation was written. Its role is to establish a concrete stochastic-data
decomposition for the thesis rather than a new confirmatory gate.

## 5.7 Post-hoc learner-conditional OEH

LC-OEH-v4 was evaluated on the already-consumed 60-MDP cohort to diagnose the
learner mismatch in v3. The estimator completed all 300 MDP--behavior groups
and 900 learner rows. The hybrid `B2 + OEH3 + LC-PMS` improved pooled
log-likelihood by 0.06215 [0.01817, 0.11513] and integrated Brier by -0.00975
[-0.01596, -0.00350]. Both predictive contrasts remained significant after
Holm correction across the screened blocks.

Its selector return was 0.69178 versus 0.68594 for best-fixed, a difference of
0.00584 [0.00001, 0.01227]. This did not survive correction across the screened
selectors (`Holm p=0.3486`). OEH-v3 alone produced the largest screened gain,
0.00908, but also missed the familywise threshold (`Holm p=0.0540`). Direct
cross-fit point-value and pessimistic selection underperformed best-fixed by
0.0444 and 0.0820.

The remaining decision mismatch was tested explicitly. A paired-advantage
regressor gained only 0.00182 with an interval crossing zero; its conservative
variant made no switches, and an inner-OOF threshold lost 0.00165. The final
OEH-v4E selector instead averages the `B2 + OEH3` and
`B2 + OEH3 + full LC-OEH4` nested-OOF return predictions with fixed weights
0.5 and 0.5. It reaches 0.69552 versus 0.68594 for best-fixed, a gain of
0.00957 [0.00244, 0.01825]. Its raw p-value is 0.0034 and its Holm-adjusted
p-value is 0.0442 across the complete family of 15 selectors. It switches
learner in 52 of 300 groups. This is a positive adaptive development result;
it does not reverse or overwrite the frozen v3 record.

## 5.8 Same-machine method panel

The compatibility panel was already run on the historical development cohort
using identical prefixes, normalized-return scoring, and the common multiplier
10/seed-0 slice. It includes CFQI, IQL, BC, the unchanged finite-MDP
Pi_b-SPIBB author core, a documented VI-LCB port, the discrete BCQ author
learner, and d3rlpy discrete CQL.

|---|---:|---:|---:|

| Tabular IQL | 0.5084 | 0.6520 | 0.2000 |

The neural runs used an RTX 3050 Ti Laptop GPU, one seed, and 500 fixed updates.
The exact tabular solvers used CPU/NumPy because the small dynamic-programming
operations did not benefit from the available GPU stack. This panel checks
implementation compatibility and gross ranking on common data; it is not an
equal-tuning-budget SOTA comparison and is not evidence for the OEH-v3 gate.

## 5.9 Gate decision

The post-freeze experiment supports three conclusions. First, OEH-v3 resolves the
observed mechanical saturation defect: it yields varied pilot-only risk
requirements without reading outcome labels. Second, the frozen B3--B2 primary
gate and threshold-probability selector fail, so mechanical repair alone is
insufficient. Third, learner-conditional features and decision-aligned return
prediction recover positive predictive and selection results on the adaptive
development analysis, including correction across all screened selectors. The
sealed evaluation split was not opened, so the result is not yet independent
confirmation or a SOTA claim.
