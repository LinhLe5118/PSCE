4. **Scientific claim:** the internal positive simultaneous bounds establish a scoped within-generator result. The completed fresh BRIDGE transfer is mixed, including unfavorable IQL scores. There is no established literature-wide SOTA claim.

## Manuscript reporting rule after completion

The original [stochastic effective-horizon study](https://aima.cs.berkeley.edu/~russell/papers/iclr24-horizon.pdf) reports the number of BRIDGE tasks solved and compares **online** learner sample complexities by Spearman correlation and median ratio on jointly solved tasks. Its task eligibility and online endpoint are explicit. Our final paper and thesis will use the same transparency principle on our own **offline** endpoint: one forecast table per panel and learner with MDP count, identical-row count, censoring, integrated Brier, seven-class log loss, paired OEH-minus-comparator MDP mean, descriptive interval, and measured runtime. The opened 14-task panel must say `exploratory` in its table caption. A separate scalar-rank table will state eligible rows and any true-MDP/reference access for original ERI/TQ/SACo/EH scores. Neither table will silently pool internal and BRIDGE tasks, convert online solved counts into offline forecast wins, or omit negative learner rows. The existing pipeline figure must continue to separate pilot-only prediction from independent learner training used to create labels.
