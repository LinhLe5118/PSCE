# The three final tests

Generated by `python -m scripts.reproduce.final_tests_doc` from the committed artifacts; do not edit by hand.

Every test uses the same frozen method: the specification [`oeh_v2_spec.json`](../../artifacts/week8/oeh_v2_development_2026-10-14/oeh_v2_spec.json) (SHA-256 `6f78edec6e8b…`, frozen 2026-10-14 16:25 UTC), the heads fitted on the 720 meta-training MDPs, and the single entry point `scripts/analysis/oeh_pipeline.py`.

## Rules common to the three tests

- The protocol (sources, sample sizes, comparators, scores, decision rule, bootstrap seed) was frozen and hashed before any data of the test existed.
- For each source, the pilot-only inputs and every forecast were written and hashed in `preoutcome_seal.json` before any learner was trained on the test data; `outcome_run.json` links the labels to that seal.
- A label is the first budget from which at least 8 of 10 runs reach a normalized return of 0.95 and keep it at every larger budget, or `>x50`.
- Scores are averaged within each MDP, then across MDPs. A contrast is the score of the comparator minus the score of OEH, so a positive value favours OEH.
- Bounds are simultaneous one-sided 95% lower bounds over the contrasts of a family (max-t with the original standard error, 10,000 resamples of whole MDPs). A family passes (GO) only if every lower bound is above zero.
- A defect found after the freeze but before any outcome is fixed only by a recorded pre-outcome amendment.

## What the evidence supports

- Against generic pilot statistics (B2, the same heads on the 18 statistics), OEH is better in the registered comparisons of Test 1 and Test 3. On Test 2 its log loss is lower than B2's, but that comparison was not registered there and its IBS difference cannot be separated from zero.
- Against the strongest baseline that also uses pilot diagnostics (the robust head), the advantage depends on the source: Test 1 passed, with most of its margin from the forest model; Test 2 did not pass.
- On Test 3, the full head alone, without the gate, scored better than OEH (descriptive, not registered).
- Every MDP is small and tabular (at most 256 state–action pairs); each label is itself estimated from ten runs on a grid of six budgets.
- The ablation, net-cost, learner-level and calibration analyses were run after the labels were opened; they are post hoc. `python -m scripts.reproduce` recomputes them.
