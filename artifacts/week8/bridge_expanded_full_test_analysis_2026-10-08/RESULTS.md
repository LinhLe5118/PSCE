# Expanded BRIDGE panel: full OEH transfer

Five published BRIDGE MDPs (two Atari, two MiniGrid, one Procgen); offline pilot and E_l labels generated by this project under the frozen prelabel protocol. All methods and failures are retained.

`brier` is the mean squared error of the six cumulative event probabilities (ordinal Brier/RPS). Lower Brier and log loss are better.

TQ/SACo use the authors' formulas and DQN reference code retrained on our sticky, reward-scaled adapter; they are not the authors' published task scores.
